I'm Albert, an M.S. Computer Science student at **Georgia Tech**, where I research trustworthy robotic foundation models in the **Trustworthy Robotics Lab**. My work focuses on understanding and improving the robustness and controllability of Vision-Language-Action and World-Action Models.

At **Mach Industries**, I work on machine learning for visual localization: making models more robust to changes in imagery, building data and evaluation pipelines, and delivering models to edge devices. I also have a background in guidance, navigation, and control, from state estimation and flight simulation to fixed-wing control and system identification.

Previously, I researched stochastic planning and control for autonomous racing in Georgia Tech's **Dynamics and Control Systems Laboratory**, and led autonomy development for **GT Design-Build-Fly SAE Advanced Class**.

I'm interested in building autonomous systems that remain reliable beyond the conditions they were trained and tested on.
